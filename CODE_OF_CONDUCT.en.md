# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, **regardless of** age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

**Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Prioritizing the interests of the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, email addresses) without
  explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## This project's specific context

This project operates in the field of **reverse engineering and security
research**. That field is inherently susceptible to misuse, so we add the
following:

- **Contributions we do not accept**: finished tools for circumventing DRM, code
  that generates keygens or crack patches, code that evades AV detection, or any
  feature whose purpose is attacking third-party systems.
- **What we do discuss**: analysis methodology, format parsing, deobfuscation,
  and defensive analysis of malicious samples.
- **Do not paste binaries of real malware** into issues or pull requests, and do
  not paste unauthorized proprietary code. Provide a hash, a pointer to where the
  sample can be obtained, or a minimized synthetic reproduction.
- When discussing vulnerabilities, follow the private disclosure process in
  `SECURITY.md` rather than opening a public issue.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour
and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
any unacceptable behaviour. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and also when an individual
is officially representing the project in public. Examples of representing the
project include using an official email address, posting via an official social
media account, or acting as a designated representative at an online or offline
event.

## Reporting

If you experience abuse, harassment or other unacceptable behaviour, contact the
maintainers privately using the contact details on the repository owner's
profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated **promptly and
fairly**.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of
any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will determine consequences using the following ladder:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining the nature of the
   violation and why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning plus a ban on interaction with the people involved
   for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — a ban on any form of interaction with the project for a
   specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban on any form of public interaction with
   the project.
